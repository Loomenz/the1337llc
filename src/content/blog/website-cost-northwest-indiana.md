## Why town pages matter here

People in the Region search by town. "Roofer Crown Point." "Salon Valparaiso." "Plumber near Munster." If your site only says "serving Northwest Indiana," Google has a hard time showing you for any one of those searches.

A page for each town you work in, written about the work you actually do there, is one of the most useful things a local site can have. It's why the Pro site includes them.

## The monthly costs nobody mentions

Whoever builds your site, ask about these before you say yes:

1. **Domain:** about $15 to $25 a year. Make sure it's registered in **your** name.
2. **Hosting:** anywhere from free to $50 a month. Ask who controls the account.
3. **Business email:** Google Workspace is about $7 per person a month.
4. **Edits:** what happens when your hours, prices or services change? Is it a phone call or a new invoice?

## How to compare quotes

When you have two or three quotes in hand, ask each one:

- **Do I own the site, the domain and the photos if I stop paying you?** If the answer is anything but yes, walk away.
- **Will it load fast on a phone?** Most of your customers will see it on one.
- **Will you set up my Google Business Profile?** In local search, that matters as much as the website.
- **Is the price fixed in writing?** A quote that "starts at" a number usually ends somewhere else.
